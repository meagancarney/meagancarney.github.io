TEDx Talk

Using machine learning to advance medical diagnosis

December 2024 · TEDxUQ 2024 ‘Pulse: Rhythm of Change’

In this talk, Dr. Carney explores how machine learning algorithms can be applied to medical decision-making, demystifying the AI techniques that are transforming how we approach diagnosis and patient care. The presentation bridges the gap between abstract mathematical methods and their tangible impact on healthcare.

Women in Science Podcast

17 September 2024 · Hosted by Dr. Kirsty Short (UQ)

Dr. Carney discusses her journey in mathematics and how she uses machine learning and extreme value theory to make informed decisions and navigate increasingly complex, dynamic systems—from predicting hurricanes to analysing immune responses.

Public Event

BrisScience: Science Comedy Spectacular!

6 November 2023 · The Edge, South Bank, Brisbane

A panel-style science comedy event examining the top science stories of 2023—and some of the quirkier ones too. Hosted by Dr. Joel Gilmore, the evening brought together researchers for a lighter look at science in the style of popular panel shows, featuring competition, entertainment, and comedy.
